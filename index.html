Top Tools for Everyday Tasks

The most-used tools on Toolyfi cover the practical, everyday tasks that come up constantly in life and work — financial calculations, productivity utilities, and quick data conversions that would otherwise require several separate apps or websites.

The QR Code Generator is the most-used tool on the platform. BMI Calculator

Sales Tax Calculator

Sales tax rates in the US vary by state, county, and sometimes by specific municipality — from 0% in Oregon, Montana, New Hampshire, and Delaware to over 10% in certain Tennessee and Louisiana counties when local taxes are combined. The Sales Tax Calculator accepts any percentage input and calculates the final price instantly, making it useful for online shoppers, small business owners, and anyone verifying a receipt or estimating total purchase cost before checkout.
