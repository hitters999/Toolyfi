Gold Rate in Lahore Today — Complete Guide 2026

Lahore is Pakistan's cultural capital and home to one of the country's most historic gold markets. The gold rate in Lahore today follows the national benchmark set by Karachi's Sarafa Jewellers Association, with local adjustments by the Lahore Sarafa Association each morning. Whether you are shopping in Anarkali's ancient bazaars, investing at Shah Alam Market, or browsing premium boutiques on MM Alam Road — understanding Lahore's gold rate is essential.

📍 Key Fact

Lahore's Anarkali gold market is one of South Asia's oldest continuously operating bullion markets — with roots in the Mughal era, over 400 years old. Pakistan's second most important gold hub after Karachi's Jodia Bazaar.

Anarkali Bazaar vs. Shah Alam — What's the Difference?

Anarkali Bazaar is the retail hub — families, brides, investors buying coins. Shah Alam Market is the wholesale hub — goldsmiths, bulk traders, raw gold. For lowest prices closest to spot rate, Shah Alam is the answer. For variety, design, and atmosphere, Anarkali is unbeatable.

How Gold Rate is Determined in Lahore

The London Bullion Market Association (LBMA) sets the international benchmark twice daily. Pakistan's Sarafa associations convert this through the USD/PKR interbank rate, add 3% import duty, apply association premiums, and announce the tola rate. Lahore's rate is typically within PKR 100–200 of Karachi's rate.

Lahori Bridal Gold — What to Buy

Lahore is famous for extravagant weddings. Traditional Punjabi bridal gold typically includes:

  • Mehndi: Light 18K bangles, choora (1–1.5 tola) — colorful, not heavy
  • Baraat: 22K full set — haar, jhumke, teeka, kangan (2.5–4 tola). Anarkali kundan is the classic choice
  • Walima: Gold coin shagun — Punjabi riwaj for the bride

Pro tip from Anarkali dealers: visit on Tuesday or Wednesday mornings for relaxed shopping and better negotiation on making charges. Avoid peak wedding season (October–December) when making charges rise 10–15%.

Seasonal Gold Trends in Lahore

Peak season (October–December): Wedding season drives highest demand. Making charges at maximum. Pre-Eid: Surge in gift purchases and new jewelry. Summer (June–August): Slowest period — best time to negotiate making charges. Dealers are most flexible when weddings are few.

How to Verify Gold Purity in Lahore

  • Karat stamp: 999 (24K), 916 (22K), 875 (21K), 750 (18K) — on clasp, inner band, or surface
  • Free acid test: Any Anarkali dealer will test for free — insist before buying
  • XRF digital tester: Available at Liberty Market and Gulberg stores
  • Always get a receipt showing weight, karat, gold value, and making charges separately
