Gold Rate in Karachi Today — Complete Guide 2026

Karachi is the financial capital of Pakistan and home to the country's largest and most active gold market. The gold rate in Karachi today is the benchmark rate that sets prices for all of Pakistan. Every morning, the Sarafa Jewellers Association in Karachi's Jodia Bazaar announces the official daily gold rate — a price then adopted by gold dealers, jewelers, and bullion traders across Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, and every other city in the country.

As of today, the 24 karat gold rate in Karachi per tola is PKR 503,270. For 22 karat gold — the most popular choice for jewelry in Pakistan — the rate is PKR 461,330 per tola.

📍 Key Fact

Karachi's Sarafa Bazaar in Jodia Bazaar is Pakistan's primary bullion market. The rate announced here every morning is treated as the official national gold rate and followed by all cities.

Karachi Sarafa Bazaar — Pakistan's Largest Gold Market

The Sarafa Bazaar in Karachi, located in the historic Jodia Bazaar area near M.A. Jinnah Road, is the beating heart of Pakistan's gold economy. The Karachi Sarafa Jewellers Association announces the official daily gold rate each morning around 10:00–10:30 AM, Monday through Saturday. This rate is immediately circulated to jewelers and dealers across Pakistan — setting the national benchmark.

Where to Buy Gold in Karachi

Jodia Bazaar — For Investors and Bulk Buyers

Jodia Bazaar is Karachi's wholesale gold market. Making charges here are minimal — often just PKR 500–800 per tola. For serious gold investors, a direct relationship with a Jodia Bazaar dealer provides access to rates closest to international spot prices.

Tariq Road — For Bridal Jewelry and Variety

Tariq Road is one of Pakistan's most popular jewelry shopping destinations. Dozens of reputable jewelers offer 22K and 21K gold in a wide range of designs. Making charges typically range from PKR 1,200 to PKR 2,500 per gram.

Clifton and Defence — Premium and International Brands

For buyers who prefer branded, certified gold jewelry, Clifton and DHA offer numerous options in malls like Dolmen Mall and Ocean Mall. Full certification, easy exchange policies, and guaranteed purity.

⚠️ Buyer's Warning

Always insist on a receipt showing: weight in grams, karat purity, gold value, and making charges — separately. Never pay for making charges on the full weight.

How to Verify Gold Purity in Karachi

  • Check the Karat Stamp: 999 (24K), 916 (22K), 875 (21K), 750 (18K)
  • Ask for an Acid Test: Any reputable dealer in Jodia Bazaar or Tariq Road will conduct a free acid test.
  • Electronic Gold Tester: Many modern jewelers in Clifton and DHA use digital testers for instant purity readings.
  • Hallmarking: Buy from shops registered with the Sarafa Jewellers Association Karachi.
