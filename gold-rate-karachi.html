Gold Rate in Karachi Today — Complete Buyer's Guide 2026

Karachi is the financial capital and economic heartbeat of Pakistan — home to the country's largest and most active gold market. The gold rate in Karachi today is the benchmark rate that sets prices for all of Pakistan. Every morning, the Sarafa Jewellers Association in Karachi's historic Jodia Bazaar announces the official daily gold rate — a price then immediately adopted by gold dealers, jewelers, and bullion traders across Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and every other city in the nation.

As of today, the 24 karat gold rate in Karachi per tola is PKR 503,270. For 22 karat gold — the most popular choice for jewelry in Pakistan — the rate is PKR 461,330 per tola. For everyday investors and families in Karachi, these morning Sarafa announcements are a daily ritual that guides purchasing decisions across the nation.

📍 Why Karachi Matters for Pakistan's Gold

Karachi's Sarafa Bazaar in Jodia Bazaar is Pakistan's primary bullion market and international gateway. The rate announced here every morning at ~10:30 AM is treated as the official national gold rate. Traders in Multan, Peshawar, and Quetta await Karachi's announcement before setting their own shop prices.

Karachi Sarafa Bazaar — Pakistan's Largest Gold Market

The Sarafa Bazaar in Karachi, located in the historic Jodia Bazaar area near M.A. Jinnah Road in the old city, is far more than a market — it is a cultural institution. Every morning, before the city wakes, traders in Jodia Bazaar are already monitoring international gold prices to predict the day's Pakistani rate.

The Karachi Sarafa Jewellers Association announces the official daily gold rate each morning around 10:00–10:30 AM, Monday through Saturday. This announcement is immediately circulated via SMS and WhatsApp to jewelers across Pakistan. By 11 AM, a bride-to-be in Rawalpindi knows the exact cost of her wedding jewelry. By noon, an investor in Faisalabad has made their decision.

Where to Buy Gold in Karachi

🏛️ Jodia Bazaar — For Investors & Serious Buyers

Jodia Bazaar is Karachi's wholesale gold market where investors can buy gold at rates closest to international spot prices. Making charges here are minimal — often just PKR 400–800 per tola. A seasoned gold investor in Karachi will have direct relationships with trusted dealers spanning decades.

🛍️ Tariq Road — For Bridal Jewelry & Variety

Tariq Road is one of Pakistan's most beloved jewelry shopping destinations and the emotional heart of Karachi's gold culture. Dozens of reputable jewelers offer 22K and 21K gold in every conceivable design. Making charges typically range from PKR 1,500 to PKR 2,500 per gram, with easy exchange policies.

🏢 Saddar — Traditional Designs & Antique Gold

Saddar is Karachi's oldest jewelry district. Jewelers here specialize in traditional koftgari (inlay work), kundan jewelry, and vintage pieces. Making charges are mid-range (PKR 1,200–1,800 per gram), but the craftsmanship is unparalleled.

🌊 Clifton & Defence — Premium & Branded

For those who prefer international brand names, Clifton and DHA offer certified gold jewelry with full certification and money-back guarantees. Making charges are highest (PKR 2,000–4,000/gram) but peace of mind has a price.

💡 Pro Tip: The Karachi Gold Buyer's Playbook

Step 1: Check the rate here. Step 2: Visit Jodia Bazaar to confirm. Step 3: Decide your budget and karat. Step 4: Pick your area. Step 5: Always get a receipt. Step 6: Negotiate making charges down by 10–15%. Step 7: Never rush — gold is a long-term investment.

How to Verify Gold Purity in Karachi

  • Check the Karat Stamp: 999 (24K), 916 (22K), 875 (21K), 750 (18K)
  • Ask for an Acid Test: Any reputable dealer will conduct a free test in front of you
  • Electronic Gold Tester: Modern jewelers use digital testers for instant readings
  • Hallmarking: Buy only from registered Sarafa Association members
