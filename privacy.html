1. Overview

Welcome to Toolyfi ("we," "us," or "our"). Toolyfi operates the website toolyfi.com, a free online tools platform providing utilities such as image resizers, QR code generators, unit converters, calculators, and more.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website. By accessing or using Toolyfi, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy. If you disagree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the site.

✅ Key principle: Most Toolyfi tools process data entirely within your browser. We do not transmit or store your tool inputs on our servers.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

For All Users

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of personalized advertising and analytics tracking

For European Union / UK Users (GDPR)

  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests

Our legal basis for processing your data is legitimate interest (site analytics, security) and consent (advertising cookies).

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@toolyfi.com. We will respond within 30 days.
