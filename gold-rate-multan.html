Gold Rate in Multan Today — Complete Market Guide 2026

Welcome to the most comprehensive guide to tracking the gold rate in Multan today. Known as the "City of Saints" and the economic capital of South Punjab, Multan boasts a gold market deeply entrenched in centuries of tradition, agricultural wealth, and unique local craftsmanship. Whether you are a Zamindar arriving from Lodhran post-harvest, or a modern family shopping in Gulgasht Colony's air-conditioned boutiques, understanding Multan's gold market is essential before spending a single rupee.

As of today, the 24 karat gold rate in Multan per tola is PKR 503,270. For 22 karat gold — universally used for bridal jewelry across South Punjab — the price stands at PKR 461,330 per tola. While this base rate is synced with the national Karachi benchmark, the true cost of purchasing gold in Multan depends heavily on where you shop and what craftsmanship you demand.

📍 Key Fact — The Hussain Agahi Advantage

Hussain Agahi Bazaar is the wholesale center of South Punjab's gold trade. High volume means dealers operate on high turnover, not high margins. Making charges (jadai) here are generally PKR 400–800 per gram cheaper than Gulgasht Colony or Bosan Road malls.

Multan's Primary Gold Markets: A Deep Dive

1. Hussain Agahi & Sarafa Bazaar — The Historic Core

Located near the Ghanta Ghar (Clock Tower), Hussain Agahi is one of Pakistan's oldest and most legendary commercial centers. Inside lies Andhi Khui — the manufacturing and bullion trading hub where raw gold is melted, refined, and crafted. Best for heavy traditional 22K bridal sets, pure 24K gold pathis, and buyers seeking the lowest making charges through firm negotiation.

2. Gulgasht Colony — Modern Premium Sector

Gulgasht Colony answered the demand for comfortable, luxurious gold shopping. Elegant boutiques with air conditioning and imported designs cater to elite families and modern brides. The base gold rate is the same, but making charges are noticeably higher for the brand premium and ambience.

3. Multan Cantonment (Saddar)

The Cantonment offers organized retail with strict security, catering primarily to military families. Fixed-price structures, excellent customer service, and post-purchase support like resizing and polishing make it ideal for security-conscious buyers.

The Art of Multani Gold: Meenakari Tradition

You cannot discuss gold in Multan without mentioning Multani Meenakari — the ancient art of fusing vibrant enamel colors onto pure 22K gold. Multan's craftsmen are nationally renowned for their intricate work, predominantly using deep blues, rich greens, and vibrant reds. Heavy bridal sets featuring large Jhumkas, Gulubands (chokers), and broad bangles are masterfully painted with this enamel.

When calculating total cost, remember that authentic Meenakari work requires exceptional labor. Making charges on enameled sets will be slightly higher than plain gold sets, but the cultural and aesthetic value is unmatched in Pakistan.

The Economics of South Punjab's Gold Market

Multan is the nucleus of Pakistan's most fertile agricultural belt, surrounding Shujabad, Lodhran, Vehari, Khanewal, and Muzaffargarh. Following major harvest seasons — particularly the summer mango and autumn cotton seasons — Zamindars receive massive cash payouts immediately converted into gold. This creates highly predictable seasonal rushes in Hussain Agahi.

💡 Multan Buyer's Tip: Timing Your Purchase

If you are budget-conscious, purchase gold during the off-season (late winter or early spring). Avoid buying immediately after mango or cotton harvests when rural liquidity is highest and jewelers are less willing to negotiate on jadai (making charges).

How the Multan Gold Rate is Calculated

  1. International Bullion (LBMA): The global price in USD per ounce is the starting point.
  2. USD to PKR Parity: Rupee strength directly dictates the local price.
  3. Karachi Benchmark: The Karachi Sarafa Association calculates the base national rate including import duties.
  4. Multan Sarafa Association: Local leaders receive the Karachi rate each morning and announce it city-wide — mirroring the national rate with a negligible PKR 50–100 variance.

How to Verify Gold Purity in Multan

  • Karat Stamp: 999 (24K), 916 (22K), 875 (21K), 750 (18K) — check inside bands, on clasps, on bangle inner surfaces.
  • Acid Test: Free at any Hussain Agahi dealer. A drop of nitric acid reveals purity through color reaction.
  • XRF Machine: Modern jewelers in Multan have digital XRF testers. The machine should confirm 91.6% purity for 22K.
  • Weigh Yourself: Bring a digital scale or ask the dealer to weigh in front of you.
  • Demand Itemized Invoice: Date, grammage, karat, daily rate, making charges — all separate line items.

Multan vs. Lahore — Which Market is Better?

Historically, South Punjab families traveled to Lahore's Shah Alam or Liberty Market for major bridal purchases. Today that is largely unnecessary:

  • Identical base rate — 24K and 22K prices are the same in both cities.
  • Lower making charges — Hussain Agahi consistently beats Lahore's premium markets on jadai.
  • Better traditional designs — For authentic Seraiki Meenakari sets, local Multan artisans execute far better than Lahore jewelers who favor lighter, cosmopolitan styles.
  • Verdict: Unless you specifically need certified 18K diamond-polki jewelry (where Lahore excels), Multan offers superior value for 22K bridal gold.

Investment Gold in Multan

For investors and Zamindars looking to preserve wealth without paying making charges, 24K pure gold is the standard. In Multan, purchase as local Pathis (smelted bars) from trusted bullion dealers in Andhi Khui, or as sealed imported Pamp Suisse/ARY bars which carry a slight premium but guarantee international certification. Store in bank lockers at HBL, UBL, or MCB branches across Multan for annual fees of PKR 3,000–8,000.
