🎯 Complete Guide to Percentage Calculations

Percentages are fundamental to mathematics, finance, and everyday calculations. This comprehensive guide explores everything you need to know about percentage calculations, including formulas, practical applications, and advanced techniques.

What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The symbol % represents "per cent" or "out of 100". For example, 50% means 50 out of 100, or half of something. Percentages are used universally in finance, statistics, science, education, and business.

Basic Percentage Formula

Formula: (Part / Whole) × 100 = Percentage

Example: If you scored 85 marks out of 100, your percentage is (85/100) × 100 = 85%

Percentage Change Calculation

Formula: ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) × 100 = Percentage Change

This is crucial for tracking growth, decline, inflation, and market changes. If a product's price increased from $50 to $75, the percentage increase is ((75-50)/50) × 100 = 50%.

Real-World Applications

  • Finance: Interest rates, loan payments, investment returns, tax calculations
  • Business: Profit margins, discount percentages, sales growth, market share
  • Education: Grade calculations, test scores, attendance rates
  • Health: BMI calculations, nutrition facts, body composition
  • Science: Concentration levels, experimental yields, error margins
  • Statistics: Probability, survey results, data analysis

Advanced Formulas

  • Percentage Markup: Original Price × (1 + Markup%) = Final Price
  • Percentage Discount: Original Price × (1 - Discount%) = Final Price
  • Compound Percentage: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)
  • Percentage Difference: |Value1 - Value2| / ((Value1 + Value2) / 2) × 100
