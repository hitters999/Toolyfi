How It Works

The tool analyzes your image using Sobel edge detection to find boundaries, then uses flood-fill algorithms to identify and isolate the main subject. The background pixels are made transparent, giving you a clean PNG. Because this runs on your local hardware, the speed depends on your device's processing power.

Tips for Best Results

  • High Contrast: Images where the subject clearly stands out from the background work best.
  • Good Lighting: Well-lit photos produce cleaner edges.
  • Simple Backgrounds: Solid or minimal backgrounds give the best results.
  • Image Quality: High-resolution images process better than low-quality photos.
