How to Play
  1. Roles: Each player receives a card. Most players are Civilians who know the secret location. One player is the Spy who doesn't know the location.
  2. Questioning: Players take turns asking each other questions about the location. Civilians want to prove they know the location without revealing it to the Spy.
  3. Spy's Goal: The Spy tries to blend in and figure out the location from the questions and answers.
  4. Voting: At any time, players can vote to eliminate someone they suspect is the Spy. If majority agrees, that player is eliminated.
  5. Spy Wins: If the Spy correctly guesses the location or time runs out, the Spy wins.
  6. Civilians Win: If the Civilians correctly identify and eliminate the Spy, they win.
Tip: Be vague but convincing! Give hints that only someone at the location would understand.
