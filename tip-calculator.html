How to Use This Tip Calculator

Our tip calculator makes it easy to calculate how much to tip at a restaurant, cafe, or any service. Enter your bill amount, choose a tip percentage, and split between any number of people instantly.

How Much Should You Tip?

  • 10% — Minimum, for average service
  • 15% — Standard, for good service
  • 18–20% — Great service (most common in USA)
  • 25%+ — Exceptional service

Tip Calculator for Groups

Use the "Number of People" stepper to split the total bill evenly. Each person's share including tip is shown instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip at a restaurant? +

Standard tip is 15-20% in the USA. 15% for good service, 18-20% for great service, and 25%+ for exceptional service.

How do I split a bill between people? +

Enter the total bill, choose your tip percentage, then set the number of people. Our calculator divides the total evenly.

Is the tip calculator free? +

Yes, Toolyfi tip calculator is 100% free with no signup required.
