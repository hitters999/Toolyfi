How to do BISP 8171 online registration 2026?

▼

BISP registration is done in person only at your nearest BISP Tehsil Office — there is no genuine online registration. Bring your original CNIC, family documents, and proof of income. Any website or agent claiming to offer BISP online registration is a scam. Report them to 0800-26477 immediately.

What is the BISP helpline number?

What to do if BISP check shows "not eligible"?

What is BISP 8171?

8171 is the official Government of Pakistan short code for the Benazir Income Support Programme. It serves both as an SMS check number and the identifier for the official web portal. Any Pakistani citizen can use it to check their BISP eligibility and payment balance for free.

  • BISP Web Portal: Go to 8171.bisp.gov.pk and enter your 13-digit CNIC to view eligibility and payment details.
  • Free SMS: Send your 13-digit CNIC to 8171. Get an instant reply — works on Telenor, Jazz, Ufone, Zong, and Warid.

BISP January 2026 Payment — Key Facts

The BISP January 2026 quarterly payment is Rs. 13,500 per registered family. Payments are distributed through HBL, Bank Alfalah, JazzCash (in select areas), and BISP Divisional offices. Once your payment is disbursed, you receive an SMS notification. Bring your original CNIC to collect from any designated payment point.

BISP Eligibility Criteria 2026

  • Household monthly income below Rs. 50,000
  • No government employee or pensioner in the household
  • Registered in the National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER)
  • No ownership of significant agricultural land or urban property
  • No four-wheeled vehicle owned in the household

BISP 8171 Online Registration 2026 — Can You Register Online?

No. BISP registration cannot be done online. It must be done physically at a BISP Tehsil Office. Any website, WhatsApp message, or social media post claiming to offer BISP online registration is a scam. Always verify by calling 0800-26477 before trusting any registration service.

How to Identify the Official BISP Portal

The only official BISP web portal is 8171.bisp.gov.pk — a verified .gov.pk government domain. Many fake sites copy its design to deceive citizens. Always check the full URL carefully. The BISP government portal is free and never asks for payment.

Avoiding BISP Scams in 2026

  • Never pay anyone to check your status or register you for BISP
  • Do not share CNIC or bank details with unknown callers claiming to be from BISP
  • Ignore WhatsApp or Facebook messages offering BISP registration links
  • Report any fraud to the free BISP helpline 0800-26477 immediately

Other BISP Programmes 2026

BISP runs several programmes beyond the Kafalat cash transfer: Waseela-e-Taleem (education stipends for children), Nashonuma (nutrition support for mothers and infants), Sahara (support for persons with disabilities), and interest-free microloans for small businesses. Check your eligibility for all programmes by calling 0800-26477 or visiting your nearest BISP office.
