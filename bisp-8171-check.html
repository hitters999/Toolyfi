BISP 8171 Check Online by CNIC — Complete Guide 2026

The Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) is Pakistan's largest unconditional cash transfer initiative, providing Rs. 13,500 quarterly to over 10 million low-income families across the country. If you or someone in your family wants to check eligibility for BISP payments, this complete guide explains every method available in 2026.

What is the 8171 BISP Portal?

The 8171 web portal (8171.bisp.gov.pk) is the official online system launched by the Government of Pakistan under the Benazir Income Support Programme. It allows citizens to check their BISP eligibility status, verify payment information, and track quarterly installments without visiting government offices.

The portal is designed to be accessible to all Pakistanis, including those in rural areas. By simply entering your 13-digit CNIC number and completing a captcha, you can instantly see whether your household qualifies for financial assistance.

How to Check BISP 8171 Online by CNIC

There are three official methods to check your BISP eligibility in 2026:

  • Method 1 — Online Portal: Use the tool above to enter your CNIC number, then visit 8171.bisp.gov.pk, enter your CNIC, complete the captcha, and click Check (Maloom Karein)
  • Method 2 — SMS: Open your phone's messaging app, type your 13-digit CNIC number without dashes, and send it to 8171. You will receive a reply within seconds. This service is completely free.
  • Method 3 — BISP Office: Visit your nearest BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC for in-person verification and assistance

BISP 8171 Payment Amount 2026

The current BISP Kafalat quarterly payment is Rs. 13,500 for eligible families. This amount is disbursed every three months at BISP camp sites, partner banks, and authorized mobile cash centers. Special relief packages can include an additional Rs. 25,000 for flood-affected or crisis-impacted families.

BISP Eligibility Criteria 2026

To qualify for BISP, your household must meet the following conditions according to the National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER) database:

  • Total household income below Rs. 50,000 per month
  • No government employee or pensioner in the household
  • No ownership of luxury property, vehicles, or large businesses
  • Registered in the NSER survey at a BISP Tehsil Office
  • PMT (Proxy Means Test) score below the defined poverty threshold

Priority is given to female-headed households, widows, divorced women, elderly women, persons with disabilities, and disaster-affected families.

What Does Each BISP Status Mean?

When you check your BISP status via the portal or SMS, you may receive one of these responses:

  • Eligible (اہل): Your household qualifies and payment will be disbursed at your nearest BISP camp site
  • Under Verification (تصدیق جاری ہے): BISP is cross-checking your data with NADRA and NSER databases — wait for the final decision
  • Not Eligible (نا اہل): Your household does not currently meet the poverty criteria — you can visit a BISP office for data correction or re-survey
  • Payment Ready: Your quarterly installment has been released and is available for collection

Common Reasons for Not Being Eligible

If your BISP check returns a "not eligible" result, common reasons include outdated NSER data, income above the threshold, a government employee in the household, or data entry errors during survey registration. You can resolve most issues by visiting your nearest BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC and supporting documents.

Warning: Beware of Fake BISP Portals

Thousands of fake websites and agents claim to offer BISP online registration or "fast-track" processing in exchange for fees. The Government of Pakistan has officially confirmed that BISP registration is only possible physically at BISP Tehsil Offices. The 8171 SMS service and 8171.bisp.gov.pk are the only official channels. Never share your CNIC or pay any fee to any website, app, or agent claiming to register you for BISP.

If you encounter fraud, report immediately to the BISP helpline: 0800-26477 (free call).

BISP Programs Under the Umbrella

The Benazir Income Support Programme includes several sub-programs targeting different vulnerable groups. The Benazir Kafalat Program provides the core Rs. 13,500 quarterly cash transfer to eligible women. Benazir Nashonuma supports pregnant women and newborns for nutrition. Benazir Taleemi Wazaif provides educational stipends for children to encourage school attendance. The Undergraduate Scholarship Program assists higher education students from low-income families.

Conclusion — Check Your BISP Status Now

Checking your BISP 8171 eligibility is quick, free, and easy. Use the tool above to enter your CNIC, visit the official portal, or send your CNIC via SMS to 8171. If you face any issues, visit your nearest BISP Tehsil Office or call the free helpline 0800-26477. Never pay anyone for BISP registration or checking — it is always free.
