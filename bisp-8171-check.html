Complete Guide to BISP 8171: Check Eligibility, Status & Benefits

The Benazir Income Support Program (BISP) is Pakistan's largest social protection initiative, providing financial assistance to deserving families across the country. With millions of beneficiaries, understanding how to check your BISP eligibility and status is crucial. This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know about BISP, 8171 check system, and how to access your benefits.

What is BISP 8171?

BISP 8171 is a USSD-based toll-free service that allows Pakistani citizens to instantly check their Benazir Income Support Program registration and eligibility status. By dialing *8171# from any mobile phone registered in your name, you can get immediate information about your BISP status without requiring internet or visiting an office.

This innovative system was introduced to make BISP services accessible to everyone, including those without smartphones or internet connectivity. The service is available 24/7 and works on all mobile networks in Pakistan including Jazz, Zong, Telenor, and Warid.

Who is Eligible for BISP?

BISP eligibility is determined through the National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER), which conducts surveys to identify deserving Pakistani families. Certain categories receive priority eligibility:

Widows: Women whose husbands have passed away and don't have sufficient means of livelihood. Widows receive priority in BISP enrollment and receive higher quarterly payments.

Disabled Persons: Individuals with physical or mental disabilities registered with disability authorities. BISP provides crucial financial support to disabled persons and their families.

Elderly Persons: Citizens aged 65 and above without adequate pension or family support. The elderly receive consistent quarterly payments to meet their basic needs.

Deserving Families: Families with monthly household income below the poverty line as determined by NSER data. Income limits vary by province and are regularly updated.

How to Check Your BISP Status Using 8171

Step 1: Take any mobile phone registered in your name. The phone must be registered with your CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card) number.

Step 2: Dial *8171# and press the call button. You'll be connected to the BISP verification system automatically.

Step 3: Follow the automated voice instructions. The system will ask for your CNIC number.

Step 4: Enter your CNIC number using your phone's keypad. The system will verify your information.

Step 5: You'll receive your BISP status within seconds. The system will display whether you're registered, eligible, ineligible, or pending verification.

Step 6: Save the information displayed. You can request a second call to hear the information again if needed.

Understanding Your BISP Status Results

Registered and Eligible: This means you've been verified as eligible for BISP benefits. You'll receive quarterly payments to your registered bank account or through other payment methods. Payments typically range from PKR 5,000 to PKR 13,000 depending on your category.

Registered but Under Review: Your registration is confirmed, but your eligibility is still being verified. This usually takes 3-6 months. Check your status again after this period.

Ineligible: You don't meet BISP eligibility criteria based on current income levels. However, you can apply for reconsideration if your circumstances change.

Not Registered: Your information isn't in the NSER database. You can visit a BISP registration center to register yourself.

BISP Payment Details and Schedule

BISP disburses benefits quarterly, meaning payments are made four times yearly. Payment amounts vary based on beneficiary category and program tier. Regular beneficiaries receive PKR 5,000 per quarter, while vulnerable categories like widows and disabled persons may receive higher amounts up to PKR 13,000 per quarter.

Payments are deposited directly into registered bank accounts or through designated payment points in areas without banking facilities. BISP coordinates with banks and mobile money operators to ensure timely disbursement.

How to Register for BISP

If you're not yet registered, visit your nearest BISP registration center. You'll need your CNIC, proof of residence, and contact information. BISP staff will conduct a survey to assess your eligibility based on income and family circumstances.

Alternatively, BISP regularly conducts surveys in different areas. When surveyors visit your neighborhood, they'll assess your eligibility automatically based on National Socio-Economic Registry criteria.

Important BISP Information and Updates

BISP regularly updates eligibility criteria and payment amounts based on inflation and economic conditions. It's important to stay informed about these changes. Follow official BISP announcements through official government channels.

Never share your personal information with unauthorized persons claiming to process BISP benefits. BISP never charges fees for registration or benefit disbursement. Be cautious of scams offering to guarantee BISP eligibility for payment.

Frequently Asked Questions About BISP

Can I check BISP status online? Yes, use our tool on this page or dial 8171. The online tool provides the same verified information as the USSD service.

How often are payments made? BISP payments are made quarterly. Once registered and eligible, you'll receive payments four times per year.

Can my status change? Yes, if your income increases significantly, you may become ineligible. Conversely, if you face hardship, you can reapply for consideration.

What documents do I need for BISP registration? You need your CNIC, proof of residence (utility bill), and information about your household income and composition.
