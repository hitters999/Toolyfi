What is a Word Counter?

A word counter is a free online tool that counts the number of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in any piece of text. It also calculates estimated reading time, which is useful for bloggers, students, and content writers.

Who Uses a Word Counter?

  • Students \u2014 checking essay word limits for assignments
  • Bloggers \u2014 ensuring articles meet minimum SEO word count
  • Writers \u2014 tracking progress on novels or scripts
