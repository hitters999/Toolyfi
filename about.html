Our Story

Toolyfi started with a simple frustration: why do basic online tools require sign-ups, subscriptions, or constant upsells? We believed that tools like image resizers, QR generators, unit converters, and calculators should just work — instantly, for free, for everyone.

What started as a small collection of utilities has grown into a comprehensive platform with over 100 tools, serving users across the UK, USA, and beyond. We're constantly expanding based on what our users actually need.

Our platform is built with a focus on speed, privacy, and simplicity. Most of our tools process data entirely within your browser — meaning your files and data never leave your device.
