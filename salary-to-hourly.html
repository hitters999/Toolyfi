How to Convert Salary to Hourly Rate

Our salary to hourly calculator converts your annual salary to an hourly wage using the formula: Hourly Rate = Annual Salary ÷ (Hours per Week × Weeks per Year). For a standard 40-hour work week and 52 weeks, divide your salary by 2,080.

Salary to Hourly Examples

  • $40,000/year = $19.23/hour
  • $60,000/year = $28.85/hour
  • $80,000/year = $38.46/hour
  • $100,000/year = $48.08/hour

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert salary to hourly rate? +

Divide your annual salary by total work hours per year. For standard 40hr/week x 52 weeks = 2080 hours. So $60,000 / 2080 = $28.85/hour.

How much is $50,000 a year per hour? +

$50,000 per year equals $24.04 per hour based on a 40-hour work week and 52 weeks per year.

How much is $100,000 a year hourly? +

$100,000 per year is approximately $48.08 per hour based on a standard 40hr/week, 52 weeks/year schedule.
