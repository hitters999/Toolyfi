Gold Rate in Faisalabad Today — The Complete Market Guide 2026

If you are searching for the gold rate in Faisalabad today, you have arrived at the right place. Faisalabad — historically known as Lyallpur and celebrated as the "Manchester of Pakistan" — operates one of the most dynamic and high-volume gold markets in the entire country. The city's extraordinary wealth, generated through textile exports, power-loom industries, and surrounding agricultural zones, fuels a gold economy that rivals Karachi and Lahore in raw transaction volume.

As of today, the 24 karat gold rate in Faisalabad per tola stands at PKR 503,270. The 22 karat rate — the backbone of traditional South Asian bridal jewelry — is PKR 461,330 per tola. These base rates follow the national benchmark announced by the Karachi Sarafa Jewellers Association and are relayed each morning to the Faisalabad Sarafa Jewellers Association before markets open at Aminpur Bazaar.

What makes the gold price in Faisalabad particularly interesting is the city's unique dual economic engine. Unlike Karachi (port and finance) or Lahore (culture and retail), Faisalabad's gold demand is cyclically tied to two powerful forces: the textile export payment cycle and the agricultural harvest season. Understanding these cycles helps you buy smarter.

📍 Aminpur Bazaar Advantage — The Wholesale Secret

Aminpur Bazaar is Faisalabad's wholesale gold hub near the iconic Clock Tower. Because transaction volumes are enormous, dealers operate on razor-thin margins. Making charges (jadai) here are typically PKR 500 to PKR 1,200 per gram lower than D Ground boutiques. On a 5-tola bridal set, this can mean savings of PKR 30,000 to PKR 70,000.

What Is the Current Gold Rate in Faisalabad?

The gold rate in Faisalabad today is updated daily and represents the base price of pure gold at the Faisalabad Sarafa market. The rate you see on this page is the raw gold value — the price per tola, per gram, and per 10 grams — for each of the four main karats: 24K, 22K, 21K, and 18K.

This base rate does not include making charges (jadai), which are added by the jeweler for the labor of crafting the jewelry. The making charge is entirely negotiable and varies significantly across Faisalabad's different gold markets. By knowing the raw gold rate, you can separate the gold value from the making charge and negotiate effectively.

The rate is synchronized with the Karachi Sarafa benchmark, which itself is derived from the international LBMA (London Bullion Market Association) price in US Dollars, converted at the open-market USD/PKR rate, plus applicable customs duties and taxes on imported gold.

Faisalabad's Primary Gold Markets: A Deep Dive

Unlike many cities where gold trade is concentrated in one street, Faisalabad's prosperity has created distinct gold market zones serving completely different buyers. Here is your insider guide:

1. Aminpur Bazaar Sarafa — The Wholesale Powerhouse

Branching off from the historic eight-road Clock Tower (Ghanta Ghar), Aminpur Bazaar is the city's oldest, highest-volume, and most prestigious wholesale Sarafa market. Hundreds of jewelers operate side by side in tight lanes, creating fierce price competition that ultimately benefits the buyer.

Best for: 24K pure gold biscuits (pathis) for investment, heavy traditional 22K Punjabi bridal sets, wholesale purchases above 2 tolas. This is where factory owners, Zamindars, and serious buyers come. Making charges are the lowest in the city and highly negotiable, especially on large orders.

2. D Ground, Peoples Colony — The Luxury Experience

As Faisalabad's industrial elite grew wealthier, they sought a more premium shopping environment. D Ground in Peoples Colony became the upscale answer. Air-conditioned boutiques showcase intricate Turkish, Dubai, and Singaporean-inspired designs alongside certified 18K diamond jewelry sets.

Best for: Modern brides, industrialist families, premium buyers who prioritize aesthetics and comfort. The base gold rate is identical to Aminpur Bazaar, but making charges are significantly higher, treated as a premium for design, branding, and ambiance. Customer service is excellent, and XRF purity testing is standard.

3. Rail Bazaar and Karkhana Bazar

These markets, also radiating from the Clock Tower area, are the middle ground of Faisalabad's gold trade. Established family jewelers serve the city's massive middle class. Designs are slightly more contemporary than Aminpur Bazaar, but making charges are much more approachable than D Ground. Ideal for everyday 22K jewelry — bangles, rings, light chains.

4. Kohinoor City and Jaranwala Road

Faisalabad's "mall culture" jewelry experience. Stores here cater to corporate professionals and mall-going families. Fixed prices, certified purity, modern packaging. No negotiation expected, but transparency is guaranteed.

The Economics of Gold in Faisalabad — Why Rates Move Differently Here

The gold market in Faisalabad is not just a reflection of international gold prices. It has its own supply-demand dynamics rooted in two powerful cycles:

The Textile Payment Cycle

Faisalabad handles a significant share of Pakistan's textile and apparel exports. Factory owners and yarn traders regularly receive large foreign-currency payments (in USD). In Pakistan's economic environment, where the PKR has historically depreciated, physical gold is treated as the most reliable store of value. Immediately after clearing large export invoices, industrialists often walk into Aminpur Bazaar and buy 24K gold biscuits worth millions of rupees in a single transaction. This institutional, non-wedding-driven demand keeps Aminpur Bazaar perpetually active year-round.

The Agricultural Harvest Influx

Faisalabad is geographically surrounded by some of Punjab's most fertile farmland — touching Chiniot, Jhang, Toba Tek Singh, and Gojra. When wheat is harvested in April–May and cotton/sugarcane in October–December, Zamindars (landowners) receive massive cash payouts. Culturally, converting agricultural profit into 22K gold jewelry for upcoming winter weddings is a deeply ingrained tradition. During peak harvest-to-wedding seasons, Aminpur Bazaar experiences intense demand spikes. Making charges rise, delivery times extend, and negotiating leverage shifts toward the jeweler.

Faisalabad Gold Rate vs. Lahore — Should You Travel?

Many wealthy Faisalabadis debate whether to drive two hours on the M-3 to Lahore (Liberty Market or Shah Alam) for better gold deals. Here is the honest comparison:

  • Base Gold Rate: Identical in both cities — follows the same national Karachi benchmark.
  • Making Charges: Aminpur Bazaar in Faisalabad is generally cheaper than Lahore's Liberty Market for traditional heavy sets. Shah Alam in Lahore offers similar wholesale rates to Aminpur Bazaar.
  • Design Range: Lahore has a slight edge in highly cosmopolitan, designer, and polki-fusion pieces targeted at upper-class Delhi-style aesthetics.
  • Verdict: For standard 22K Punjabi bridal sets and 24K investment gold, there is absolutely no financial reason to leave Faisalabad. Aminpur Bazaar delivers exceptional value. Travel to Lahore only for extremely niche designer pieces not available in D Ground.

How to Buy Gold Safely in Faisalabad — Step by Step

Step 1: Check Today's Rate on This Page

Never enter a gold shop without knowing the current rate. Use our live calculator above to establish the pure gold value for your desired weight.

Step 2: Understand the Two-Part Price

Total Price = Pure Gold Value + Making Charges (Jadai). The gold value is a market-determined number — non-negotiable. The jadai is 100% negotiable and varies by shop and location.

Step 3: Insist on XRF Purity Testing

Reputable jewelers in Faisalabad now have XRF (X-ray fluorescence) computerized testing machines. Insist your jewelry is tested in front of you. A 22K piece should read 91.6% (marked "916") purity. Never accept only a verbal assurance or a traditional acid test (kasi) for large purchases.

Step 4: Get a Proper Invoice

A legitimate Faisalabad Sarafa invoice must include: date, item description, exact weight in grams, karat purity (e.g., 22K / 916), daily base rate applied, making charges per gram, total amount, and the shop's buyback policy. This receipt is your legal protection.

Step 5: Carry Gold Safely

For purchases exceeding 3–4 tolas, many established jewelers in Aminpur Bazaar will provide a security escort to your vehicle. Increasingly, buyers complete the deal in-person but transfer payment via RTGS bank transfer or PayOrder instead of carrying millions in cash.

Investment Gold in Faisalabad: Biscuits vs. Branded Bars

For the industrialist community parking capital in gold, 24K pure gold is the only viable form. Here are your two options in Aminpur Bazaar:

  • Local Pathis (Biscuits): Locally smelted 24K bars, typically sold in multiples of 10 tolas. Traded heavily between industrialists. Carry zero premium but require established trust with your dealer and careful documentation of purity.
  • Imported Branded Bars: PAMP Suisse, Valcambi, or ARY sealed bars. Come with assay certificates and are globally recognizable. Carry a slight 1–2% premium but are universally accepted without dispute upon resale.
