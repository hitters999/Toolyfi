The Complete Guide to US Sales Tax in 2026

Navigating the US sales tax system can be a daunting task for both consumers and business owners. Unlike many countries with a national Value Added Tax (VAT), the United States has a decentralized system where each state, and often each city or county, sets its own rates. Our Free Sales Tax Calculator simplifies this complexity, providing instant calculations for all 50 states.

US Sales Tax: State-by-State Breakdown

While some states have high rates to fund public services, others have no sales tax at all. Here are the key categories for 2026:

States with NO Sales Tax

Five US states do not collect any state-level sales tax, making them popular destinations for major purchases:

  • Delaware: The most famous tax-free state.
  • Montana: No state or local sales tax.
  • New Hampshire: No sales tax on most goods.
  • Oregon: A major tax-free hub on the West Coast.
  • Alaska: No state tax, though some local municipalities may charge small fees.

States with the Highest Rates

These states have the highest base rates, though local taxes can push these even higher:

  • California: 7.25% (Base)
  • Indiana, Mississippi, Rhode Island, Tennessee: 7.00%
  • Minnesota: 6.875%
  • Nevada: 6.85%
  • New Jersey: 6.625%

Understanding Local vs. State Tax

The rate you see in our dropdown is the State-Level Rate. However, in many states, cities and counties add their own "Local Option" taxes. For example:

  • New York: The state rate is 4%, but in New York City, the total sales tax is 8.875% due to local additions.
  • Illinois: The state rate is 6.25%, but in Chicago, the total can exceed 10%.
  • Texas: The state rate is 6.25%, but most cities add 2%, bringing the total to 8.25%.

Tax-Free Items & Sales Tax Holidays

Many states offer exemptions for specific categories of goods to reduce the burden on families:

  • Groceries: Most states do not tax "unprepared" food items.
  • Prescription Drugs: Almost all states exempt necessary medications.
  • Clothing: Some states (like Pennsylvania and New Jersey) do not tax most clothing items.
  • Sales Tax Holidays: Many states (like Florida and Texas) have "Back to School" weekends where sales tax is waived on clothes and school supplies.

E-commerce & The "Wayfair" Rule

If you are an online business owner, you must be aware of Economic Nexus. Following the 2018 Supreme Court ruling (South Dakota v. Wayfair), states can require online retailers to collect sales tax even if they don't have a physical presence in that state, provided they meet certain sales thresholds (usually $100,000 in sales or 200 transactions per year).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the tax higher at the store than on this calculator?
Our calculator uses the base state rate. Your local city or county likely adds an additional 1-4% on top of the state rate.
Do I have to pay sales tax on online purchases?
Yes, most major online retailers (like Amazon) now collect sales tax based on your shipping address. If they don't, you are technically required to pay "Use Tax" to your state.
Which state has the lowest sales tax?
Excluding the five states with 0% tax, Colorado has the lowest state-level rate at 2.9%.
Is there a national sales tax in the USA?
No. The US federal government does not collect a national sales tax. All sales taxes are managed at the state and local levels.
Do tourists have to pay sales tax?
Yes, everyone who makes a purchase in a taxable state must pay the tax. Unlike some European countries, the US does not have a widespread system for tourists to claim tax refunds at the airport.
Are services taxable?
This varies by state. Some states (like Hawaii and New Mexico) tax almost all services, while others (like California) generally only tax tangible goods.
What is "Use Tax"?
Use tax is a tax on the storage, use, or consumption of a taxable item or service on which no sales tax was paid. It's designed to prevent people from avoiding tax by buying out-of-state.
How do I calculate tax manually?
Multiply the price by the tax rate (as a decimal). For example, $100 x 0.07 = $7.00 tax. Total = $107.00.
Do I pay tax on shipping costs?
This depends on the state. Some states consider shipping part of the taxable sale, while others exempt it if it's listed as a separate line item.
What is a Resale Certificate?
A document that allows businesses to buy goods tax-free if they intend to resell them to customers (who will then pay the sales tax).
