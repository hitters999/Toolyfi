What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measurement that uses your height and weight to estimate if you are at a healthy weight. It is used by doctors and health organizations worldwide to screen for weight-related health risks.

💡 BMI CATEGORIES

Underweight: under 18.5 | Normal: 18.5–24.9 | Overweight: 25–29.9 | Obese: 30 and above

Pro Tips

  • BMI is a screening tool — not a diagnostic tool
  • Athletes may have high BMI due to muscle mass — not fat
  • Use BMI alongside other health measurements for best results

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI range?
A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered normal/healthy for most adults.
Is BMI accurate?
BMI is a useful screening tool but does not directly measure body fat. Athletes with high muscle mass may have elevated BMI without being overweight.
