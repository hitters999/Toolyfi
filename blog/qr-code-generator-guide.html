What is a QR Code?

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a 2D barcode that smartphones can scan to instantly open a website, save contact info, connect to WiFi, or access any digital content. QR codes are essential for marketing, business cards, menus, and product packaging.

💡 KEY TAKEAWAY

QR codes are scanned over 10 billion times per month globally. Adding a QR code to your print materials is one of the fastest ways to connect offline audiences to your online content.

QR Code Pro Tips

  • Always test your QR code before printing by scanning with your phone
  • Use a link shortener first to keep URLs clean and trackable
  • Make QR codes at least 2cm x 2cm for reliable scanning
  • Add a call-to-action near the QR code — "Scan to learn more"
