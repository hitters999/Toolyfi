What is an AI Text Detector?

An AI text detector analyzes written content and determines the probability it was generated by artificial intelligence like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini — rather than written by a human.

As AI writing tools become more common, detecting AI-generated content has become critical for teachers, editors, HR managers, and content managers.

💡 KEY TAKEAWAY

AI detectors work by analyzing perplexity (predictability) and burstiness (sentence variation). AI text is very uniform — human text is more varied and natural.
