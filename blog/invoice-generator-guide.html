What to Include in a Professional Invoice

  • Invoice Number — Unique ID for tracking (e.g., INV-001)
  • Invoice Date & Due Date — When issued and payment deadline
  • Your Business Info — Name, address, email, phone
  • Itemized List — Description, quantity, unit price
  • Tax & Total — Clear breakdown of all amounts
  • Payment Terms — Bank details or payment method
