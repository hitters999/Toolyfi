3 Ways to Improve Your Writing Quality

1. The "20-Word" Rule: Aim for an average of 15–20 words per sentence. If our sentence counter shows a high word-to-sentence ratio, your text may be too hard to read on mobile.

2. Whitespace is King: For web content, keep paragraphs under 4 lines. This reduces "eye fatigue" and keeps readers scrolling.

3. Check the Reading Time: Most readers drop off after 7 minutes. Use our Read Time metric to ensure your content is punchy and efficient.
