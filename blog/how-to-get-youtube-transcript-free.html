What is a YouTube Transcript?

A YouTube transcript is the complete written text of everything spoken in a video. YouTube automatically generates captions for most videos using speech recognition. These captions can be saved as a text transcript.

Transcripts are used for note-taking, creating blog posts, translation, SEO, and accessibility. Instead of watching a 30-minute video twice, you can read its transcript in 3 minutes.

Method 2: YouTube's Built-in Transcript Feature

YouTube has a hidden built-in transcript feature. It is free but requires manual copying and has no AI analysis.

  1. Open the YouTube video in your browser
  2. Click the three-dot menu (…) below the video
  3. Select "Open transcript" from the dropdown
  4. The transcript panel appears on the right side
  5. Click inside the panel, select all (Ctrl+A), then copy (Ctrl+C)
  6. Paste into Notepad, Google Docs, or anywhere you need it

⚠️ Limitation: YouTube's built-in transcript includes timestamps and messy formatting. It also provides no summary or AI analysis. For a cleaner result, use Toolyfi's extractor instead.

Method 3: Google Docs Voice Typing

This method works for videos with no captions. Google Docs can transcribe audio in real time using your microphone.

  1. Open a new Google Doc
  2. Go to Tools → Voice typing (or press Ctrl+Shift+S)
  3. Play the YouTube video out loud through your speakers
  4. Google Docs will type the spoken words in real time
  5. Review and clean up the text when done

💡 Best used as a last resort. This method is slower, less accurate, and requires manual cleanup. Use Method 1 for any video that has YouTube captions.
