What is a YouTube Transcript?

A YouTube transcript is the full text version of everything spoken in a video. It includes timestamps and can be used for studying, content creation, accessibility, and more.

💡 Key Takeaway

Transcripts save hours of manual note-taking. You can extract key insights, quotes, and summaries from any YouTube video in seconds.

Method 2: YouTube's Built-in Transcript Feature

YouTube has a built-in transcript feature — but it's hidden and only works on videos that have captions enabled.

1

Open the Video

Go to YouTube and open the video you want the transcript for.

2

Click Three Dots (···)

Below the video title, click the three-dot menu icon.

3

Select "Show Transcript"

Click "Show transcript" from the dropdown menu.

4

Copy the Text

The transcript panel opens on the right — select all and copy.

⚠️ Limitation

This only works if the video creator has enabled captions. Many videos don't have this option available.

Method 3: Google Docs Voice Typing

This method is manual but works for any video — even those without captions.

1

Open Google Docs

Go to docs.google.com and create a new document.

2

Enable Voice Typing

Go to Tools → Voice Typing and click the microphone icon.

3

Play the YouTube Video

Play the video — Google Docs will transcribe the audio in real-time.

⚠️ Limitation

Accuracy depends on audio quality. Background noise and accents can affect results. This is the slowest of the 3 methods.
