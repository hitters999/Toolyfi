What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a numerical value calculated from your weight and height. It's widely used as a screening tool to categorize individuals as underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese. Our BMI calculator gives you instant results in both metric and imperial units.

How is BMI Calculated?

The formula is: BMI = weight(kg) / height(m)². For imperial units: BMI = 703 × weight(lb) / height(in)². Our tool handles all conversions automatically.

Limitations of BMI

  • BMI does not measure body fat percentage directly
  • Athletes may have high BMI due to muscle mass
  • BMI interpretation differs for children vs adults
  • Consult a doctor for a complete health assessment
