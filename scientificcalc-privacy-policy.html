ScientificCalc Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Summary: ScientificCalc is designed as an offline scientific calculator. The current version does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

1. Data Collection

ScientificCalc does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The application is designed to work offline and does not require access to contacts, location, microphone, camera, files, or phone calls.

2. Analytics and Advertising

The current version of ScientificCalc does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services. If a future version adds analytics, advertisements, payments, or another third-party service, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

3. Calculator Data

Calculator expressions, results, memory values, and history are processed locally on the user’s device. The current version does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

4. Children’s Privacy

ScientificCalc is a general-purpose calculator and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version does not intentionally transmit personal information to third-party services. The app store, device manufacturer, and operating system may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Security

Because ScientificCalc does not use user accounts or a developer-hosted database, the developer does not maintain a personal-data database for this application. Users should keep their device and operating system updated and use the security controls provided by their device.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or concerns about ScientificCalc, contact umerm1992@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. Any updated version will show a new effective date. Users should review this page periodically for changes.
