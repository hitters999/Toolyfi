10 Expert Resume Writing Tips for 2026

  1. Quantify Your Impact: Instead of "Managed a team," use "Led a team of 12 to increase quarterly revenue by 24%."
  2. Keyword Optimization: Mirror the language used in the job description to rank higher in ATS scans.
  3. Reverse-Chronological Order: Always list your most recent experience first; it's what recruiters care about most.
  4. Keep it Concise: Aim for 1 page if you have under 10 years of experience, and no more than 2 pages for senior roles.
  5. Use Action Verbs: Start bullet points with strong verbs like "Spearheaded," "Engineered," or "Orchestrated."
  6. Professional Email: Use a simple name-based email address. Avoid nicknames or outdated domains.
  7. Check for Typos: A single spelling error can disqualify you. Proofread multiple times.
  8. Standard Fonts Only: Stick to fonts like Arial, Calibri, or Georgia which are safe for all systems.
  9. White Space is Your Friend: Don't crowd the page. Proper margins and spacing improve readability.
  10. Save as PDF: This ensures your formatting remains identical on every device the recruiter uses.
