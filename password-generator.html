What is a Password Generator?

A password generator is a tool that creates random, secure passwords automatically. Instead of using weak, predictable passwords like "password123", a strong random password generator creates complex combinations that are nearly impossible to guess or crack.

Why Do You Need a Strong Password?

Weak passwords are the number one cause of account hacking. Our strong password generator creates passwords using uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols — making your accounts secure against brute force attacks.

Best Practices

  • Use a different password for every account
  • Minimum 12 characters for regular accounts, 16+ for banking
  • Always include symbols and numbers
  • Store passwords in a trusted password manager
