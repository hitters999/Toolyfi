Wait — Meta Built a GPT-4 Killer in 9 Months?

I was skeptical too. OpenAI took 2+ years to get from GPT-3 to GPT-4. Google needed 18 months for Gemini Ultra. Anthropic spent 14 months on Claude 3.

Meta just did it in 9 months. And they made it free.

The part that broke Twitter: Muse Spark hit 91.3 MMLU while training on 40% less compute than Llama 3 405B. Meta's Super Intelligence Lab basically rewrote the training playbook and didn't tell anyone until launch day.

What Exactly Is Muse Spark?

Muse Spark is Meta's first model in the new "Muse" family. It's a dense transformer with 280B active parameters, but uses a hybrid mixture-of-experts router that only fires 35B params per token. That means GPT-4 level quality at 1/8th the inference cost.

Key specs:

  • Parameters: 280B total, 35B active per token
  • Context: 256K tokens input, 8K output
  • Training data: 18T tokens through March 2026
  • Multimodal: Text, image input. Video and audio coming Q3 2026
  • License: Free for use on meta.ai. Weights release Q3 2026
Key distinction: Unlike Llama, Muse Spark is not open-weight yet. Meta is keeping it closed while they stress-test safety. But inference is free and unlimited for consumers.
