What Actually Changed?

For years, AI chatbots responded to complex visual questions with static text and the occasional flat diagram. If you asked how a double pendulum behaves, you got a paragraph. If you wanted to understand orbital mechanics, you got a description.

Gemini just ended that era.

The new feature lets Gemini generate functional, interactive simulations directly in your chat window. These aren't images or screenshots — they're actual running programs you can manipulate with sliders, input fields, and controls, built in real time from your description.

The Three Things Gemini Can Now Build

1. Interactive Physics Simulations

This is the headliner. Gemini can now run live physics simulations where you control the variables in real time. Ask about the double pendulum problem — a classic example of chaotic motion — and Gemini builds a running simulation where you adjust pendulum lengths and initial angles and watch the motion change instantly.

🔬 Physics Simulations You Can Build

  • Double pendulum with adjustable arm lengths and initial conditions
  • Projectile motion with angle, velocity, and gravity controls
  • Moon orbiting Earth — adjust velocity and gravity to create/destroy stable orbits
  • Double slit experiment showing wave interference patterns
  • Spring-mass oscillation systems with damping controls
  • Wave propagation and superposition simulations
📸 Google Official — Gemini building the double slit experiment from "Show me how a double slit experiment works"

2. Rotating 3D Molecular Models

Chemistry students and researchers can now ask Gemini to visualize molecules in 3D and rotate and inspect them from any angle. Ask about caffeine, DNA, or a drug molecule — Gemini builds the 3D structure on the spot, a task that traditionally needed specialized software like PyMOL or Chimera.

3. Dynamic Interactive Charts

Not just static bar graphs — Gemini can build charts that respond to your inputs. Ask it to show how compound interest grows and it generates a chart where you adjust principal, rate, and time period live. How to Use It Right Now

The feature has started rolling out globally. Here's exactly how to access it:

1

Go to gemini.google.com

Open the Gemini app on web, iOS, or Android. Make sure you're signed in to your Google account.

2

Select the Pro Model

In the prompt bar, click the model selector and choose Gemini 3 Pro. The interactive visualization feature requires Pro or above.

3

Use the Right Trigger Words

Start your prompt with "Show me how...", "Help me visualize...", or "Simulate..." — these phrases trigger the interactive rendering mode.

4

Interact With the Result

Use the sliders and controls in the simulation to explore scenarios. Ask follow-up questions to adjust or expand it.

⚠️ Note: This feature is NOT yet available for Google Workspace and Education accounts. It's rolling out to personal Google accounts globally as of April 2026.

How Does Gemini Build These Simulations?

Under the hood, Gemini generates working HTML, JavaScript, and canvas/WebGL code in real time, then renders it directly in chat. The model maps natural language to UI components: sliders map to numerical inputs, comparisons trigger side-by-side layouts, and physics scenarios trigger canvas-based simulation engines.

Independent testing shows specific trigger phrases produce interactive outputs over 90% of the time:

🎯 Trigger Phrases That Work Best

  • "Make an interactive [calculator/simulator/board]" — 94% success rate
  • "Simulate [X] with adjustable [variables]" — 91% success rate
  • "Show me how [X] works" — 87% success rate
  • "Let me visualize [concept] interactively" — 85% success rate
  • "Create a [chart/diagram] I can adjust" — 79% success rate

The key insight: phrases like "show me," "let me," and "interactive" trigger the rendering engine. Passive phrases like "explain" or "describe" still return text-only responses in most cases.

Deep Research Gets Visual Upgrades Too

Alongside chat-based simulations, Google upgraded Gemini's Deep Research feature with rich visual outputs. Previously dense text reports now automatically generate:

  • Custom charts and bar graphs from data in the report
  • Interactive simulation models you can adjust within the report
  • Textbook-style diagrams that explain discovered concepts
  • Periodic tables, molecular models, and topic-specific visual aids
⚠️ Important: Deep Research visual outputs are currently exclusive to Google AI Ultra subscribers ($249.99/month). Basic interactive simulations in regular chat are rolling out to all users for free.
