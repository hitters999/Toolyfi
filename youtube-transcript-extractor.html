  1. Check Auto-Captions: If a video has "Auto-generated" captions, the accuracy might be 90-95%. Always do a quick scan for technical terms.
  2. Use Timestamps: Our tool includes timestamps so you can easily jump back to the video to verify a specific quote.
  3. Clean Up Text: Use our "Text Trimmer" tool (linked below) to remove extra spaces or line breaks from the extracted text.
  4. Translate: Once you have the text, you can easily use Google Translate to turn the transcript into any language.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract transcripts from private videos?
No, for security and privacy reasons, our tool only works with public or unlisted videos where you have the direct link.
Does it work in languages other than English?
Yes! If the YouTube video has captions in Spanish, French, German, or any other language, our tool will extract them exactly as they appear.
Is there a limit to how many videos I can process?
There are currently no daily limits. You can extract as many transcripts as you need for your projects.
How accurate is the transcription?
The accuracy depends on the quality of the captions provided by the video uploader or YouTube's auto-captioning system. Generally, it is very high for clear audio.
Can I download the transcript as a PDF?
Currently, we support .TXT downloads and clipboard copying. You can easily paste the text into a Word doc and save as PDF.
Does this tool work on mobile devices?
Yes, Toolyfi is fully responsive. You can extract transcripts on your iPhone, Android, or tablet just as easily as on a desktop.
What if a video has no captions?
If a video has captions disabled by the creator and no auto-captions were generated, we won't be able to extract the text.
Is this tool legal to use?
Yes, extracting transcripts for personal use, research, or accessibility is generally considered fair use. However, always respect the copyright of the original content creator.
Do you store the videos I process?
No. We only process the URL to fetch the text. We do not download or store the video files on our servers.
How does the AI Summary work?
We use a lightweight LLM (Large Language Model) to analyze the extracted text and generate a structured summary of the most important information.
