How to Extract YouTube Transcripts: A Step-by-Step Tutorial

Transcribing YouTube videos has never been easier. Follow this guide to get started:

1

Navigate to the YouTube video you wish to transcribe. Click the "Share" button and copy the URL, or simply copy the link from your browser's address bar. We support standard links, shorts, and mobile URLs.

Paste into Toolyfi Extractor
