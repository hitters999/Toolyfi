Gold Rate in Rawalpindi Today — Complete Guide 2026

Rawalpindi — "Pindi" to millions — is one of Pakistan's most important gold markets, home to the legendary Raja Bazaar Sarafa. The gold rate in Rawalpindi today follows the Karachi Sarafa benchmark, with the Rawalpindi Sarafa Association announcing the local rate at Raja Bazaar by 10:30 AM daily. What makes Rawalpindi unique is not the base rate — which is identical across Pakistan — but the significantly lower making charges at Raja Bazaar compared to any organized retail market in the country.

Key Fact — Raja Bazaar Wholesale Power

Raja Bazaar is a wholesale market. Dealers from Abbottabad, Mirpur AJK, Attock, Jhelum, and Chakwal source gold here. For purchases of 3 tolas or more, Raja Bazaar making charges (PKR 700–1,200/gram) can save PKR 10,000–30,000 versus Islamabad organized retail shops.

Raja Bazaar vs Saddar — What's the Difference?

Raja Bazaar is the wholesale heart — hundreds of competing dealers, lowest making charges, traditional bazaar culture. Requires confident negotiation but delivers Pakistan's best gold value. Saddar Bazar in the cantonment area is more organized — formal receipts standard, military clientele, slightly higher making charges but still well below Islamabad. For army families in Chaklala or GHQ, Saddar is the natural first stop.

How to Buy Gold in Raja Bazaar — Step by Step

  1. Check today's rate on this page before visiting. Know your per-gram baseline for 22K or 24K.
  2. Understand the two-part price: Gold value (weight × rate × purity) + Making charges. Always ask separately.
  3. Get weight certificate: For purchases above 2 tolas, watch the weighing yourself or use a government-certified scale at Raja Bazaar.
  4. Negotiate making charges: In Raja Bazaar these are negotiable. For 3+ tolas, dealers typically reduce PKR 100–300/gram. Cash payment may yield additional discount.
  5. Check karat stamp: 916 (22K), 999 (24K), 875 (21K), 750 (18K) — on clasp, inner ring band, or bangle surface.

Seasonal Gold Demand in Rawalpindi

Rawalpindi's gold demand follows a distinctive pattern shaped by its military establishment and regional wholesale role. Peak periods: wedding season (October–December), pre-Eid purchases, and the military promotion cycle — a uniquely Rawalpindi pattern where officer families mark promotions with gold purchases. AJK and KPK dealers increase Raja Bazaar visits during summer holidays.
