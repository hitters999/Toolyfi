Temperature Scale Definitions

  • Celsius (°C): The most widely used temperature scale in the world. Water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C.
  • Fahrenheit (°F): Primarily used in the United States. Water freezes at 32°F and boils at 212°F.
  • Kelvin (K): The absolute temperature scale used in science. Based on absolute zero (−273.15°C).

Common Temperature Reference Points

  • Absolute Zero: 0 K = −273.15°C = −459.67°F
  • Freezing Point of Water: 273.15 K = 0°C = 32°F
  • Room Temperature: 293.15 K = 20°C = 68°F
  • Body Temperature: 310.15 K = 37°C = 98.6°F
  • Boiling Point of Water: 373.15 K = 100°C = 212°F
